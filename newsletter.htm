STUDENT NOTES · ISSUE 01

Building My Technology Foundation

October 11, 2026 · By Zachary Langborgh

Connecting the pieces

My interest in cybersecurity starts with wanting to understand how computers and networks work. As a dual-enrollment student, I am building that foundation through college coursework while continuing to develop my communication and problem-solving skills. Programming, networking, and web development give me different ways to approach a technical problem. This newsletter brings those interests together and gives me a place to share what I am working toward.

Building this portfolio gives me a practical reason to work with HTML and CSS. A useful website needs more than information on a screen: its pages need clear headings, readable text, and links that help visitors find their way around. Using a shared stylesheet also connects the pages visually. As I add to the site, my goal is to keep the design consistent and make the content easy to use on both a computer and a phone.

 From coursework to practice 

My technical interests include Python, computer networking, information technology, and cybersecurity. Each area supports a different part of the same goal: understanding how systems are built and how they can be protected. I want to keep practicing the fundamentals before moving on to more advanced projects. Writing down the steps I take and checking my work are habits I want to carry into future technical work.

My next steps

My next goal is to make this portfolio a useful record of my education. As I complete more work, I plan to add examples that show both the result and the process behind it. I also want to keep improving how I explain technical ideas, because clear communication matters alongside technical knowledge. My experience with work, scouting, and community service gives me a foundation in responsibility that I can build on as a student.

  • Practice explaining how a small Python program works.
  • Connect networking concepts with real troubleshooting questions.
  • Check website navigation and readability on different screen sizes.
  • Add completed work to my portfolio as it becomes available.
