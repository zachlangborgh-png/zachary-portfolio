Personal Security Audit

Pick one account and review these questions.

  1. Do I recognize every device signed into this account? Sign out of devices you no longer use. If you see a device you don’t recognize, review your recent account activity.
  2. Are my recovery email and phone number current? Update any recovery email address or phone number you can no longer access.
  3. Do I still use every app that has access to this account? Remove access for apps you no longer use.
  4. Could I find my backup codes if I lost my phone? Store your backup codes somewhere secure that you can access without your phone.
  5. Is my sign-in protected? Set up a passkey if the service supports it. If you sign in with a password, turn on two-step verification.
  6. Have I used this password anywhere else? If so, replace it with a unique password and save it in a password manager.
  7. Have I reviewed my recent security alerts? Open the service’s app or website directly and check for sign-ins or account changes you don’t recognize.
